Robyn An is a second-year medical student at Swansea University, having previously earned a Bachelor of Arts in Psychology, cum laude, from Columbia University. She joins the MilkINESS study as a research intern, where she plans and conducts screening and research visits, collects and analyses milk and blood samples, and manages data entry for the study. She is thrilled to be part of a project bringing sensitive, community-centred research to mothers whose needs have too often gone unrecognised.
