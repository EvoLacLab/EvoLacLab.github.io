Laura Galante, PhD, is a Lecturer in Human Physiology at Swansea University and Head of the Evolutionary Lactation Lab. She holds a PhD in Biomedical Science from the University of Auckland (New Zealand), an MSc in Biology Applied to Nutritional Science from Università degli Studi di Milano, and a BSc in Biology from Università degli Studi di Trieste (Italy).

Her research programme investigates sex-specific developmental programming through maternal milk biology, combining human cohort data with a unique comparative animal model — the Asian elephant — to understand how environmental stressors and deprivation interact with offspring sex to shape lifelong health trajectories for mothers and babies. She is particularly committed to translating research findings into applied interventions and diagnostic tools that improve breastfeeding and health outcomes in marginalised and deprived communities.

She leads international collaborations across Japan, Finland, the UK, Germany, Burundi and Italy, and has authored 15 peer-reviewed publications. Find her full profile on [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Laura-Galante-2).
