## Open Positions

We currently have the following open positions in the group and welcome applications from anyone interested who has the relevant experience to cover the posts.

### 1. Clinical Research Nurse

*Kansai area, hourly contract, part-time*

- **Expected start date:** as soon as possible
- **Hours of work:** 15h/week on average, but will vary based on study needs
- **Length of the post:** up to 2 years from start date
- **Pay:** 2,000 JPY/h
- **Location:** Rinku Medical Centre

**Role overview and tasks**

We are seeking a dedicated Clinical Research Nurse for a part-time on-site position in the Kansai area (Rinku Medical Centre). The Clinical Research Nurse will be responsible for recruiting participants, coordinating and conducting clinical research visits, ensuring alignment with study protocols and ethical standards, and the collection of data (questionnaire-based and anthropometry) and samples (blood spots, milk and hair).

**Requirements**

- Japanese nursing registration.
- Proficiency in Japanese (native or JLPT N1/N2 level) and English (B2).
- Prior clinical research experience.
- A background in maternal and child nursing.
- Ability to work as part of a team.
- Excellent organization and communication skills.

{% include section.html %}

### 2. Research Technician

*Miyazaki University, part-time*

- **Expected start date:** anytime between January and June 2027
- **Hours of work:** 20h/week
- **Length of the post:** 1 year
- **Pay:** 2,250,000 JPY per year
- **Location:** Miyazaki University

**Role overview**

We are looking for a graduate-level Research Technician to join the MilkINESS study team at Miyazaki University. This role focuses on the technical handling and analysis of biological samples (milk, blood and hair) collected from study participants. The ideal candidate will have previous lab experience with the analysis of biological samples and knowledge of at least some of the techniques used in the study. Tasks include performing lab tests on study samples. Techniques used include, but are not limited to, ELISA, BCA and LC-MS. The Research Technician will also be responsible for helping with the validation of new methodology for the study.

**Requirements**

- Proficiency in English (B2 or equivalent). Japanese proficiency is not required for this position.
- Background in life sciences, biology, biochemistry, or a related field (Bachelor's degree or equivalent experience).
- Prior experience with biological sample processing and analysis.
- High level of precision and ability to follow detailed Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs).
- Team player with excellent communication skills.
